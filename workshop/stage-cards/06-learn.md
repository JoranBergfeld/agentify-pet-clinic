# Learn Stage Card

Use the [Evidence Lenses](../../docs/workshop/evidence-lenses.md) while you work.
When this card is Review ready, ask your partner to use
[Reciprocal Evidence Review](../../docs/workshop/reciprocal-evidence-review.md).

## Goal

Keep the lessons that can improve your future work.

## What can go wrong

Keeping vague ideas, short-term details, or lessons without evidence.

## What to record

- One Agentic Engineering Principle that helped.
- One risk, failure, or weak assumption you found.
- One change to use in another project, tool, or way of working.
- Your Learning Gate decision about what to keep.

it records the same lesson clearly.
